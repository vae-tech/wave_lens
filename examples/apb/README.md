# APB3: write, read with wait states, slave error

**Shows** an APB3 master (`apb_master`, its `phase` is an enum `IDLE SETUP ACCESS`) and a four-register slave (`apb_regs`): a write to 0x04, a read of 0x04 stretched by two wait states (`PREADY` low), a write to the unmapped 0x40 answered with `PSLVERR`.
**Build:** `make example EX=apb` → `$(BUILD)/examples/apb/apb.html` (from `apb.config.json`) and `apb_all.html` (the whole hierarchy, config made by `mkconfig.py`).
**Config:** written by hand — views with `"signals"` lists, `enum` for `phase`, `bookmarks` and `arrows` per view; the Makefile passes the dump with `--waves`.
**Look at:** bookmarks 1–2 and the *setup → access* arrow (SETUP and ACCESS of the write), 3–4 (wait states, read data: its `"label"` by the circle), 5–6 and *error* (PADDR → PSLVERR; 6 is placed `"at": "posedge PSLVERR"`, its time found in the dump, and its `"link": "exact"` button opens the exact view); the arrow's `{dt}` (`setup → access · 10 ns`) and the span *write* (from 45 ns to PREADY's fall); the static SVG of the write with its legend; the exact view with markers at `done` and `PSLVERR` (that one `"on"` an event object).
**Assertions:** `apb.sva` — the APB3 protocol (setup → access, stable address and data, wait states, `PSLVERR` for the unmapped address) and the master's phases, as a checker module bound to `apb_tb`; `sva parse examples/apb/apb_tb.sv examples/apb/apb.sva` (the testbench first: it declares `apb_pkg`), or `make sva-examples`, which also runs them over the dump (`sva eval --scope apb_tb`): all hold except `apb_demo_no_wait`, wrong on purpose (the reads have wait states), which fails twice with its trace. In the page: the config lists both files in `"sva"` and binds every assertion to `apb_tb` (`"bind"`), so `apb.html` opens with the assertions panel counted; on `apb_all.html` load `tests/sva/apb.sv` with *Load file…* and drag a line onto `u_regs` or `u_master` (the passive counter `u_count` has no `PSEL`: *cannot bind*). The third-party APB checkers of [`tests/sva/third_party`](../../tests/sva/third_party/README.md) run over this dump too.
